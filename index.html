Freedom Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no ads and no analytics. We can't see your money or your messages.

Who we are

Freedom Tracker is published by DM Titan LTD. You can reach us at dmtitan.official@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these in a file on your phone, and doesn't send them to us or anyone else:

  • Your name, age and country, if you enter them.
  • What you own and owe, your budget, dreams, money you lent and zakat choices.
  • Bank messages you add, and what the app reads from them: amounts, balances, shops, dates and the last four digits of a card or account.

This file is left out of Android cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers. If you lose or reset your phone, it can't be recovered unless you kept your own backup file.

Bank messages

The app doesn't ask for permission to read your SMS. It only sees a message when you paste or share it in, or when you tap the button that reads your clipboard. One-time passcodes are skipped and not saved.

What goes over the internet

The app connects to these services. It only asks for prices and fonts, and sends none of your data:

  • open.er-api.com for exchange rates.
  • api.gold-api.com for the gold price.
  • fonts.googleapis.com (Google Fonts) for the app's fonts.

Like any website, these services can see your device's internet address when it connects.

Things you choose to open

  • Send feedback opens your own email app with your message ready. We only receive it if you send it, along with your email address, the app version and the language. Please don't include account numbers or passwords.
  • Podcasts open in YouTube or Spotify, whose own privacy policies apply.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal or financial data.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We can't connect to your bank or move your money.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, and your data on the phone is erased. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

Freedom Tracker is meant for adults aged 18 and over, and isn't directed at children.

Not financial advice

The app's numbers and suggestions are estimates from what you enter, for your own planning. They aren't financial, tax or religious advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
